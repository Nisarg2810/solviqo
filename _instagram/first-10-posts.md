**The one idea behind all 10:** *your business already has software. It's a WhatsApp group, three Excel files and a guy named Ramesh who remembers everything. We turn that into one app, at a fixed price, in weeks.*

That's the unique angle. Other agencies post "5 benefits of custom software." We post the WhatsApp group.

## How the leads come in

Every post ends with exactly **one** keyword. The person comments the word and you DM them. Do it by hand for the first month (it's personal, and it starts a conversation), or set up ManyChat later.

| Keyword | What they get | Why it brings leads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SCOPE** | Our 1-page scope sheet (PDF) | Only someone thinking about building software wants it |
| **AUTOPSY** | They send their worst spreadsheet, we reply with a 60-second voice note on what app it should be | A free mini-consult that turns into a sales call |
| **FIT** | A 15-min call: "is this worth building, or should you buy something off the shelf?" | Direct booking. The honest framing gets people to say yes |
